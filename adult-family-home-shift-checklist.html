A day that is written down can run without you.

This is the daily checklist format used for the 7am to 7pm shift in a licensed 4-bed adult family home in Milwaukee. One page, one per day, signed by the lead and filed in the binder.

It does two jobs. Your staff know exactly what a finished shift looks like. And you can read one page and know the day happened, without being in the building.

Resident-specific steps belong in each resident's care plan, not on this page. Have your nurse review the medication lines before you use it.

The first-shift checklist.

7:00am handoff

  • Clocked in on-site
  • Walked the home with overnight staff and saw every resident
  • Read the overnight log
  • Controlled medications counted together; count matches
  • Door alarms on; exits clear
  • Handoff sheet signed by both

Morning

  • Morning care done per each care plan
  • Morning medications given; record signed at each pass
  • Breakfast served and noted
  • Day-program residents left on time with what they need
  • Kitchen, bathrooms and common areas cleaned
  • Monitoring logs up to date

Midday

  • Lunch served from the posted menu and noted
  • Noon medications given; record signed
  • Appointments and visitors logged
  • Activity or outing done and noted
  • Supplies checked; shopping list updated

Afternoon and evening

  • Returning residents checked in; times logged
  • Afternoon and evening medications given; record signed
  • Dinner served and noted
  • Kitchen and common areas clean; trash out
  • Medication storage locked

Before the shift ends

  • Daily note finished for every resident
  • Medication record checked line by line: no blanks
  • Incident reports written for anything that happened
  • End-of-shift report sent to the owner

7:00pm handoff

  • Walked the home with overnight staff and saw every resident
  • Controlled medications counted together
  • Residents, medications, incidents, appointments and supplies covered out loud
  • Handoff sheet signed by both
  • Clocked out on-site

Lead signature ____________________   Overnight staff signature ____________________   Date __________

An operating guide, not legal or medical advice. Wisconsin's rules for licensed adult family homes are in chapter DHS 88.

Questions owners ask.

Is this checklist required by the state?

No. Wisconsin requires trained staff and accurate records; it does not require this form. A signed daily checklist is one practical way to show the day was run correctly.

Can I copy it?

Yes. Print it, change it to fit your home, and have your nurse review the medication lines before staff use it.

Who should sign it?

The lead on shift signs each section, and both staff sign at each handoff.

How long should a handoff take?

About 15 minutes: walk the home together, see every resident, read the log, count controlled medications, sign.

What goes with the checklist?

A shift playbook, a hiring packet and a training plan for a lead. That full set is what I install in 30 days.
