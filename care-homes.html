Questions owners ask.

Who is this for?

Owners of licensed 3 and 4 bed adult family homes and small group homes in the Milwaukee area who still cover shifts, make the schedule or take the night calls.

Is this in line with Wisconsin rules?

The playbook is written around Wisconsin's rules for licensed adult family homes (DHS 88), and your nurse reviews the medication steps before any staff are trained on them. It is an operating guide, not legal advice.

I do not have a lead caregiver. Can this still work?

Yes. The hiring packet is there to find one, and many owners already have a caregiver who can step up once there is a real training plan.

I am not good with technology.

If you can text, you can use this. The daily documents your staff sign are paper. The AI tools work in the background and you approve everything they draft.

Do you see resident records?

No. Resident records stay in your home. I work from your routines and your schedule, not your charts.

What does it cost?

The fit check is free. The Floor Audit is $197. The 30-day Owner Off the Floor install is $2,500, and the $197 is credited if you start within 14 days of the audit. You can split the install into two payments.

Where do you work?

In person in Milwaukee, Waukesha, Racine and Kenosha counties. Owners elsewhere in Wisconsin can work with me by video.
